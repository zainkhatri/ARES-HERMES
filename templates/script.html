Panel questions — Q&A after the demo

They hold questions until after the demo. These will all come at once. Stay calm. For anything you don't know: "Why does that matter to you?" or "Let me get that answer for you after this."

Most likely
"Why not just extend our Tableau investment?"
"Tableau is great at presenting answers to questions you already know. The problem you described — your merchandiser needing an answer in the meeting, not four days later — is exactly where Tableau creates the bottleneck, not solves it. Your BI team doesn't go away with Sigma. They just stop being a helpdesk."
Most likely
"We already paid for Snowflake. Why another layer?"
"That's exactly what your CIO asked us. Snowflake is world-class at storing and processing data — it's not designed to be what a merchandiser opens every morning. Sigma is what makes the investment you already made accessible to the people who need it. You're not adding a layer — you're finally getting ROI from the one you have."
Most likely
"Why not just give our power users SQL access directly to Snowflake?"
"You could — and your data team already does. The problem is your merchandiser can't write SQL. Every question they have goes through a data scientist who has 30 other requests. Sigma doesn't replace Snowflake or your data team — it lets the business reach what's already there, without going through your BI team every time."
Likely
"Can your merchandisers actually learn this?"
"If they use Excel today, they can use Sigma tomorrow. The formula syntax is identical — that's by design. We've seen merch teams self-sufficient in a week. The bigger question is what they do with the time they get back from waiting on BI."
Likely
"How does implementation actually work?"
"Sigma connects to your existing Snowflake — no migration, no data movement. Your Snowflake admin grants access to the tables you choose, and your team is live. Typical time to first workbook is days, not months. The bigger lift is change management — getting merchandisers comfortable exploring data themselves. We help with that."
Likely
"What does pricing look like?"
"Pricing is based on the number of users and the capabilities you need. Happy to put together something specific for your team size after this — that's a conversation for me and your CIO to have together. What I can say is most customers find it ROI-positive quickly because of what they're currently spending on BI bandwidth."

"Yes — every query Sigma runs is visible in your Snowflake query history. It generates ANSI SQL. If you want to pop into your Snowflake console right now I can show you the history, or we can dig into this in the technical session."

Fallback if something breaks

Don't apologize. Don't panic. Keep talking while you fix it quietly. If you can't fix it in 10 seconds, say "Let me jump to the finished version" and switch tabs.
