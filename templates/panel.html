Pushback scripts

"Why not just use SQL / connect a data scientist directly to Snowflake?"
"You could — and your data team already does. The problem isn't that Snowflake can't be queried. The problem is that your merchandiser can't write SQL. So every question she has goes through a data scientist who has 30 other requests. Sigma doesn't replace Snowflake and it doesn't replace your data team — it lets the business reach what's already there, without going through your BI team as a bottleneck every single time."
"Why not just extend our Tableau investment?"
"Tableau's great at presenting answers to questions you already know. The problem you described — your merchandiser needing to answer a supplier disruption question in the meeting, not 4 days later — that's exactly where Tableau creates the bottleneck. Your BI team doesn't go away with Sigma. They just stop being a helpdesk."
"How does our data team stay in control?"
"Your engineers still control what tables are exposed in Snowflake — same as today. What changes is that once they say 'merchandisers can see this,' the merchandiser doesn't need a data scientist to turn access into an answer. Guardrails stay. Bottleneck goes."
"We already invested in Snowflake. Why another layer?"
"That's exactly the question your CIO put to us. Snowflake is world-class at storing and processing data — it's not designed to be what a merchandiser opens every morning. Sigma is what makes everything you've already built in Snowflake accessible without an engineering degree. You're not adding a layer — you're finally getting ROI from the investment you already made."
"Are you sending our data to OpenAI?"
"No. The AI we just ran is Snowflake Cortex — a native Snowflake service. Your data never left your warehouse. Compliance teams tend to like that a lot."
"What about shadow analytics — everyone doing their own numbers?"
"Everyone's working off the same live tables in Snowflake. There's no 'download to Excel and do your own thing' escape hatch. Sigma generates SQL that runs on your warehouse — one version of truth, just more people who can reach it."
"Can your merchandisers actually learn this?"
"If they use Excel today, they can use Sigma tomorrow. The formula syntax is identical. We've seen merch teams self-sufficient in a week. The bigger question is what they do with the time they get back from waiting on BI."
